Objective
Improve the existing Crownstroke marketplace and product detail experience. On /shop, display both blank base products (customizable starting points) and actual designer listings. Designer listings must be clearly marked as artist originals and must stay non-customizable. Keep ready-made studio designs out of the shop. Blank product records should be distinguishable from designer products (e.g. isCustomizable true for blanks, false for designer work).

Aesthetic direction
Build on the site's existing Crownstroke visual language and current homepage redesign. Make the shopping experience feel like a polished independent-artist marketplace: confident editorial typography, crisp image-led cards, saturated blue/lime accent used sparingly, dark neutral canvas, clear contrast and useful mobile behavior. Preserve existing layouts and component conventions; do not rewrite unrelated pages.

Scope and requirements
1. Update src/pages/Shop.tsx to combine blank base product templates sourced from PRODUCT_DATA with database designer listings joined to their shops. Do not add READY_MADE_PRODUCTS. Clearly distinguish blank products from designer originals, preserve filters/search/sort/cart. Blank products should expose customization and open their product page/customizer path; designer products should never show a Customize action and should link to the designer's shop.
2. Improve src/pages/ProductDetail.tsx to present blank and designer products as distinct, polished product pages. Keep blanks customizable and designer products non-customizable. Add the right seller/shop attribution and route to the designer's shop when available. Improve hierarchy, image gallery, specs/options, purchase actions, responsive behavior, empty/loading states. Avoid unrelated changes.
3. Put the product mockup composition in a dedicated homepage hero-only component or clearly isolated hero markup. It should use product mockup images only in the hero section, with accessible alt text; do not leak mockup overlays/badges into product pages or catalog cards.
4. Update Product/ProductCard types and card treatment only as necessary to support blank vs artist listing and shop linking.
5. Do not alter database schema, seeded ready-made data, or designer/shop management flows.
6. Preserve the user's existing working-tree changes. Limit edits to the files needed for this request.

Acceptance criteria
- /shop displays blank T-shirt, hoodie, mug, poster/art-design, long sleeve, and tank top templates based on available PRODUCT_DATA plus real designer listings.
- Blank cards clearly invite customization; designer cards clearly identify the maker/shop and have no customization link/action.
- Product detail handles both kinds without runtime errors, allows blank customization, and gives designer originals a seller/shop trail.
- Homepage hero product/image mockup is isolated to the hero and no ready-made studio images/products appear in the shop.
- Responsive styling remains consistent with the existing app.
