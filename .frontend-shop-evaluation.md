# Evaluation — Attempt 1

## Overall Verdict: MAJOR REVISION

## Overall Assessment
The current design has a strong, consistent Crownstroke look: image-led product cards, lime signals for blank canvases, and a distinctive hero collage. Blank product pages are especially clear and attractive at desktop and mobile sizes. The designer-product path is not complete yet: an item shown in the shop opens a “Product not found” page, the artist card does not surface its shop link in the rendered UI, and one blank category has no image.

## What's Working Well
- The shop clearly separates “Blank canvas” from “Artist original”; artist cards have no Customize action while blank cards do.
- The 375px shop layout keeps a two-column grid with readable names and usable card actions.
- Blank T Shirt detail has a strong image-first desktop layout, clear color/size/quantity controls, and a visible “Customize this blank” path. Its mobile presentation also reads cleanly.
- The hero product collage is isolated in `HomeHeroProductMockup.tsx` and visually distinct from product-page and catalog cards.

## Issues Found
### Issue 1: Designer products lead to a not-found detail page
- **What**: The “Crown Stroke” designer listing is present in the shop with ID `22bdcc9d-6dec-440d-a4df-9e1f54f73273`; clicking its product link opens a page headed “Product not found.”
- **Where**: `/shop` artist card to `/product/:productId`; `ProductDetail.tsx` designer lookup.
- **Why it matters**: Buyers cannot inspect or purchase the artist product after discovering it, so the central marketplace flow is broken.
- **Suggested fix**: Make the detail lookup resolve the same designer row returned by the catalog query, and keep a proper loading state while `productId` changes. Verify a shop listing by following its actual link.

### Issue 2: Designer shop attribution/link is missing on the rendered catalog card
- **What**: The artist card is labeled “Artist original,” but it shows no “By”/shop attribution and no shop visit link. The rendered page has zero links matching `/shop/`.
- **Where**: Artist cards on `/shop` and, by extension, the link to `/shop/:shopSlug`.
- **Why it matters**: The user asked to route buyers through designers’ own shops; the shop relationship is not usable from the actual page.
- **Suggested fix**: Ensure the catalog result maps a non-empty `shopSlug`/`shopName`, then render a visible “Visit [shop]” link outside the product-detail anchor.

### Issue 3: The Art Design blank has no image source
- **What**: “Art Design Blank” renders a blank card image area; its image has an empty `src` and `naturalWidth` 0.
- **Where**: `/shop`, Art Designs category.
- **Why it matters**: The product grid looks incomplete and the user has no visual basis for selecting that blank.
- **Suggested fix**: Use a valid design-canvas mockup or fall back to the poster mockup for this category.

### Issue 4: Homepage designer showcase is empty
- **What**: The featured section displays its heading and “View all designs” but no designer cards, even while `/shop` displays an artist listing.
- **Where**: Homepage “Featured designers” rail.
- **Why it matters**: The landing page promises original designer work, then leaves its showcase area blank.
- **Suggested fix**: Reuse the successful shop-listing query/data path for the hero/featured rail and show a designed empty state only when the catalog truly has no listings.

## Priority Fixes for Next Attempt
1. Fix and verify the click-through from an actual designer listing to its product detail page.
2. Map and display the designer's real shop name and slug, with a visible Visit Shop link.
3. Give Art Design Blank a valid image and populate the homepage featured rail from the same real catalog data.

## Should the next attempt REFINE or PIVOT?
REFINE. The visual direction is sound and the blank-canvas flow is clear; the next pass should focus on resolving the designer data/link flow and the two visible empty image areas, not changing the aesthetic.
